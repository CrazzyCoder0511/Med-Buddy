The problem

You leave the doctor's office with a fast verbal explanation, a scribbled note, and a pharmacy label written in Latin abbreviations. Three days later you can't remember whether the antibiotic was with food or on an empty stomach.

Roughly half of people taking long-term medication don't take it as prescribed. Most reminder apps assume you've already translated the instructions into a tidy schedule. That translation is the hard part — and it's the part MedBuddy does.

What you get

Understands messy input Full sentences, fragments, pharmacy shorthand — it all works
Scans medicine labels Camera OCR + FDA drug info, entirely on your device
Flags what matters Overlapping doses, food conflicts, missing units, course end dates
Tracks adherence Not just what was prescribed — what you actually took
Shares with a caregiver One link gives a read-only view — your caregiver never needs an account
Finds the right doctor near you Describe your symptoms and get matched to the right specialist, then see real clinics nearby — anywhere in the world
Keeps your insurance ready Policy details and card photos on hand at the hospital counter, with a reminder before your cover expires

MedBuddy is not medical advice. It helps you remember what you were already told. Always check against your pharmacy label.
